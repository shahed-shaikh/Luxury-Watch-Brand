AURUM is a luxury watch brand website built with HTML5, CSS3, and Bootstrap 5. Its elegant typography, refined layout, and premium visual direction create a sophisticated online presence inspired by timeless design.

The homepage introduces the brand with a striking hero section and an invitation to explore the collection. Three featured timepieces—The Classic, The Heritage, and The Modern—showcase different expressions of watch design. The craftsmanship section highlights AURUM's focus on precision, thoughtful details, and a distinctive design philosophy.

The story section presents the brand's values through three themes: Vision, Design, and Purpose. The contact area invites visitors to connect with the team, while the footer provides collection links, information pages, contact details, and social links.

Built with Bootstrap 5.3.3, custom CSS, and Google Fonts, the website demonstrates responsive navigation, structured content sections, image-led presentation, and a consistent luxury-inspired visual identity. It presents a polished identity centered on elegance and craftsmanship.
